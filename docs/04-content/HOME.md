# HOME

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The homepage introduces the artist.

Not every artwork.

Not the résumé.

Not every skill.

The homepage creates curiosity.

---

# Structure

Hero

↓

Featured Artwork

↓

Collection Preview

↓

About Preview

↓

Contact

---

# Hero

Large typography.

Minimal text.

Strong first impression.

The hero should answer:

Who is this?

What do they create?

Why should I continue scrolling?

---

# Hero Copy

Maximum:

One headline.

One supporting paragraph.

One CTA.

---

# Featured Artwork

Display only the strongest work.

Quality over quantity.

Each artwork should invite exploration.

---

# Collection Preview

Show a small curated selection.

Never display every artwork.

Encourage visitors to continue.

---

# About Preview

Introduce Rodge briefly.

Focus on philosophy.

Avoid résumé details.

---

# Contact

Simple.

Minimal.

Invitation rather than advertisement.

---

# Rule

Visitors should understand the identity of the artist within five seconds.
