# COLLECTION

Version: 1.0

# Purpose

The Collection page acts as the digital gallery.

Every artwork receives equal respect.

---

# Layout

Large imagery.

Generous spacing.

Minimal interface.

---

# Categories

Digital Illustration

Graphic Design

Poster Design

Anime

Future categories remain hidden.

---

# Filtering

Simple.

Fast.

Never overwhelming.

Avoid excessive filter combinations.

---

# Search

Future feature.

Should prioritize artwork titles before tags.

---

# Grid

Allow different image proportions.

Avoid forcing everything into identical cards.

The gallery should feel organic.

---

# Hover

Reveal

Title

Category

Year

Nothing else.

---

# Pagination

Infinite scrolling is discouraged.

Prefer:

Load More

or

Pagination

Visitors should feel progress.

---

# Rule

The gallery should feel curated.

Not endless.
