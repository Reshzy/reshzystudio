---

---

Clients

---

Developers

---

Artists

---

# Visitor Mindset

Most visitors will spend less than 60 seconds on the website.

The homepage must immediately establish credibility.

Do not expect visitors to read everything.

Design for scanning first.

Exploration second.

Reading third.

---

# Desired Reactions

"I wish I made this."

"I want to know how this was created."

"This feels different."

"This person has excellent taste."

---

# Final Thought

People rarely remember every project.

They remember how the portfolio made them feel.

Design for memory, not information.
