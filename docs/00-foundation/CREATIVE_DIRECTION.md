# CREATIVE DIRECTION

Version 1.0

---

# Creative Vision

Imagine walking through a contemporary art museum.

The architecture disappears.

The artwork becomes the experience.

The portfolio should recreate this feeling digitally.

---

# Design References

The project takes inspiration from the qualities of exceptional design—not from copying specific websites.

Reference qualities include:

- Apple's clarity
- Swiss editorial layouts
- Modern museum exhibitions
- Premium fashion publications
- Minimal Japanese aesthetics
- Award-winning interaction design

The final result must remain original.

Visual clutter.

Loud gradients.

---

# Experience

The experience should feel:

Slow when observing.

Fast when navigating.

Smooth when scrolling.

Natural when interacting.

---

# Rhythm

Every page should alternate between:

Visual intensity

↓

Silence

↓

Visual intensity

↓

Silence

Whitespace creates rhythm.

Rhythm creates elegance.

---

# Visual Hierarchy

1. Artwork

2. Typography

3. Motion

4. Interface

If the interface becomes more noticeable than the artwork,

the design has failed.

---

# Timelessness

The website should still feel beautiful five years from now.

Choose restraint over trends.

Choose clarity over novelty.

Choose quality over quantity.
