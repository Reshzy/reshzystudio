# DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Every design decision must follow these principles.

---

## Principle One

The artwork comes first.

Nothing should visually compete with the artwork.

---

## Principle Two

Remove before adding.

Whenever possible:

remove

instead of decorate.

---

## Principle Three

Motion communicates.

Motion never entertains.

Animations exist to:

Guide

Reveal

Connect

Explain

Never distract.

---

## Principle Four

Whitespace is content.

Empty space creates focus.

Never fear generous spacing.

---

## Principle Five

Typography is architecture.

Words build hierarchy.

Typography should create rhythm before color does.

---

## Principle Six

Consistency creates trust.

Every interaction should feel like it belongs to the same product.

---

## Principle Seven

Details matter.

Spacing.

Animation timing.

Hover states.

Image quality.

Transitions.

Micro-interactions.

Together these create craftsmanship.

---

## Principle Eight

Performance is a design feature.

Fast websites feel premium.

Slow websites never do.

Optimize before decorating.

---

## Principle Nine

Accessibility is elegance.

Everyone should enjoy the experience.

Beautiful interfaces should also be usable.

---

## Principle Ten

Timeless over trendy.

If a trend compromises longevity,

ignore it.
