# STORYTELLING

Version 1.0

---

# Philosophy

People remember stories.

Not specifications.

Every artwork should tell a story.

---

# Story Structure

Context

↓

Challenge

↓

Idea

↓

Execution

↓

Reflection

---

# Questions Every Artwork Should Answer

What inspired this?

Who was it made for?

What emotion should it evoke?

What techniques were used?

What did the artist learn?

---

# Writing Style

Concise.

Honest.

Reflective.

Avoid technical jargon unless necessary.

Avoid overexplaining.

Let the artwork remain the main storyteller.
